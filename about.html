Behind the Studio

Hi, I'm Zhou — pattern designer & maker

Zanith Crafted Studio was born from a love of handcrafted beauty and a belief that good design should be accessible to every creator.

Based in Vietnam, I design seamless surface patterns, POD products, and digital goods — each one made with intention, ready for your next creative project.

Whether you're a maker, a small business owner, or just someone who loves beautiful things, I hope you find something here that speaks to you. 🌿

— Zhou
